## What it does

Proposal Writer AI turns discovery call notes, CRM context, and your win themes into a tailored, persuasive proposal in minutes — with pricing pulled from your quote engine and case studies matched to the prospect's industry.

## Key features

- **Discovery-to-draft** — proposals generated from call notes and CRM data.
- **Win-theme library** — messaging that wins per segment, auto-applied.
- **Case-study matcher** — relevant proof points inserted per industry.
- **Pricing integration** — live quotes embedded from [Quote Generator AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/quote-generator-ai/).
- **Engagement tracking** — see which sections prospects actually read.

## Who it's for

Sales and solutions teams spending evenings writing proposals.
